| Type | Decision |

## Outcome

Choose a primary audience and the devices, identity providers and notifications the prototypes must support.

## Blocked by

- [P0-07: Review G0 and authorize the next phases](p0-07.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Compare the named wallet and consumer-finance references using the agreed research dimensions. Record observations and source dates.
- [ ] Select one primary audience from research evidence. Define its onboarding, signing, recovery and exit tasks.
- [ ] Choose candidate launch identity providers with documented availability and terms. The later factor tests still decide which pairings are safe.
- [ ] Choose minimum iOS versions, reference devices, credential managers and browser coverage. Include users without a second device.
- [ ] Select notification events and channels for usability testing. Separate attempted delivery from guaranteed receipt.

## Evidence

An audience decision, competitive research record, provider shortlist and device/channel matrix.

## Failure or deferral

Record insufficient research or platform access as blockers. Do not invent participant results.

| Field | Value |

| Type | Gate |

## Outcome

Decide whether the charter, ownership and capacity support the Phase 1 and Phase 2 work.

## Blocked by

- [P0-01: Record the charter and assign the open decisions](p0-01.md)
- [P0-02: Establish code provenance and the Kukai reuse boundary](p0-02.md)
- [P0-03: Verify cryptographic libraries and development toolchains](p0-03.md)
- [P0-04: Fund the work and reserve independent review](p0-04.md)
- [P0-05: Prepare custody legal questions and platform constraints](p0-05.md)
- [P0-06: Freeze the initial proof obligations and review rules](p0-06.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Check that statements retain their Fact, Decision, Proposal or Open labels. Every open item has an owner and a closing phase.
- [ ] Verify the reuse and provenance decisions, legal-request delivery and dated library survey.
- [ ] Confirm named staffing, equipment access, funded work and booked audit capacity. Record the scope that this capacity supports.
- [ ] Confirm the mandatory property list is frozen before Phase 2 starts.
- [ ] Publish a pass, blocked or fail decision with links to the reviewed artifacts. Closing an issue alone does not pass the gate.

## Evidence

A signed-off G0 record and a list of unresolved blockers.

## Failure or deferral

Keep Phase 1 and Phase 2 execution blocked until G0 passes. Drafting their tickets is permitted.

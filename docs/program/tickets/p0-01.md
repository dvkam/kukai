# P0-01: Record the charter and assign the open decisions

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Phase | 0 |
| Type | Decision |
| Status | Draft |
| Owner | Unassigned |
| Required roles | Project owner and product lead |
| Decision targets | No open question closes through this ticket alone. |

## Outcome

Define the work that Phases 0 to 3 must settle before the team builds wallet infrastructure.

## Blocked by

No ticket dependencies. Execution still requires an owner and acceptance of the draft.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Confirm the new-wallet scope, minimum curves, release platforms and unilateral-exit requirement. List the accepted exclusions.
- [ ] Give every open question and risk a named owner, a closing phase and a linked ticket. Keep Q-13 and Q-14 deferred.
- [ ] Record audience candidates and hypotheses without presenting them as research results. Recover the referenced 16 research dimensions or document the missing source.
- [ ] Record what counts as an accepted decision and who reviews security, product and legal outcomes. Separate project decisions from reviewer recommendations.
- [ ] Keep plan v2 and its dependency ledger outside the backlog. Record proposals that still require approval.

## Evidence

An accepted charter, an owner register and a decision log linked to the baseline.

## Failure or deferral

Leave unanswered decisions open. Missing owners block G0; ticket publication does not close a question.

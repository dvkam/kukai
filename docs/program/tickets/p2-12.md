## Outcome

Decide whether the chosen architecture has enough evidence to enter the Phase 3 admission experiments.

## Blocked by

- [P2-10: Prove the architecture properties and test the assumptions](p2-10.md)
- [P2-11: Receive legal advice for the selected custody design](p2-11.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Verify the custody, recovery, signing and operational decisions. Confirm every Phase 2 question is closed, including the BLS selection or deferral.
- [ ] Check proofs for all non-waivable architecture properties and accepted exceptions for eligible properties. Keep their statuses distinct.
- [ ] Verify minimum-curve recovery, compromise response and independent-exit experiments on the required platforms.
- [ ] Confirm C2 feedback has been resolved and the selected design has the required legal opinion.
- [ ] Record pass, blocked or fail with evidence. List any optional key type deferred from Phase 3.

## Evidence

A G2 decision, question-closure checklist and evidence index.

## Failure or deferral

Failure of a minimum-curve requirement blocks G2. Optional-curve deferral does not excuse a missing minimum-curve result.

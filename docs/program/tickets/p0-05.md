# P0-05: Prepare custody legal questions and platform constraints

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Phase | 0 |
| Type | Research |
| Status | Draft |
| Owner | Unassigned |
| Required roles | Project owner, qualified legal reviewer and iOS lead |
| Decision targets | No open question closes through this ticket alone. |

## Outcome

Request advice on the proposed signing service and identify release constraints before the team chooses a custody design.

## Blocked by

- [P0-01: Record the charter and assign the open decisions](p0-01.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Describe A1 and B, user-held quorums, social identity, recovery, signer control and unilateral exit in the legal brief.
- [ ] Record the intended operator jurisdiction, launch markets and data flows. Ask the owner for facts that the repository cannot establish.
- [ ] Prepare the written request and have the authorized owner send it to qualified counsel. Preserve a redacted delivery reference for G0.
- [ ] Review current Apple rules for wallets, social sign-in and an in-app browser. Define an early TestFlight check and list required accounts and devices.
- [ ] Track required advice and platform access as external dependencies. Keep legal conclusions separate from engineering opinions.

## Evidence

A legal brief, delivery reference, platform checklist and questions for the Phase 2 opinion.

## Failure or deferral

If sending or paid engagement lacks authorization, mark it as an owner action. This draft does not authorize contacting counsel or purchasing services.

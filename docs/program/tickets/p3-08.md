## Outcome

Determine whether the measured signing, recovery and exit flows meet the product targets.

## Blocked by

- [P3-05: Validate device storage and compromise responses](p3-05.md)
- [P3-06: Demonstrate unilateral exit with our services blocked](p3-06.md)
- [P1-04: Review G1 and retain the C2 and C3 follow-ups](p1-04.md)

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Measure the minimum curves against the G1 device, network and sample conditions. Report raw samples and the required percentiles.
- [ ] Compare complete onboarding, returning login, signing, recovery and exit tasks. Separate provider delay from cryptographic and application delay.
- [ ] Assess the selected custody design's effect on web-only users, confirmation frequency and comprehension.
- [ ] Record pass or fail against the original targets. Any revision requires a reason and approval before a new validation run.
- [ ] Deliver C3 and return findings that contradict custody or scope to their decision owners.

## Evidence

A C3 handover, measured results and accepted target or scope changes with history.

## Failure or deferral

Do not raise thresholds after a failed run and relabel that run as passing. Missing physical-device data remains unrun.
